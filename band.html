Earth Band (beta)

Three NASA visualizations play together as one band, each read live from its own video, on one beat and one scale. Lightning is the drums, ocean currents the bass, night lights the melody. Every note comes from a frame; the pads only switch parts on, off, or thinner.

An artistic remix. These three visualizations cover different years and are played together, not as simultaneous events.

Only the bass is on to start. Switch on the drums or the melody to add them.

Pads

Arrow keys move between pads. Space or Enter switches a pad. Hold Solo to hear one member alone, and let go to return. Escape stops all sound.

What each member plays

    Pan is left and right on each member's own map, pitch is height on its map within that member's range, loudness is how much. One scale for all three: A minor pentatonic. The bass note follows the warm share of the water, scaled to the range it actually covers in this clip (35.7 to 40.0 percent), so the bass moves as the seasons turn.

    Not an official NASA product. A sonification of NASA visualizations, not of the raw data; values read from colors are approximate.

    Visualizations: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (5011, 5313); NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio (3912). Clips reduced in size and frame rate for the band.
