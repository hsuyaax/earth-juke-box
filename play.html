        How it works

        Scales inspired by ragas (experimental). A raga is more than a scale, and nobody trained in Hindustani music has reviewed this. With one on, a quiet drone holds the home note: a fixed reference tone, not data. Shown on records with a melody.

        Keys: Space play or pause · 1–9, 0 pick a record · O what the engine sees · arrows explore the map · D describe · Esc stop all sound

        Live: natural events from NASA EONET

        This is not a warning system. The feed can lag by hours or days. For warnings, follow your local authorities or GDACS. Events show as red diamonds on the lightning, aerosol, and Earth Today maps; the probe plays a short alert when it touches one.
